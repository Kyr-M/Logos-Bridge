# The Dyslexia Project

> 🏆 **Winner of The New Now : health** innovation competition (2021)

An assistive technology project focused on **improving reading comprehension for people with dyslexia**.

---

## 🏆 Award

The Dyslexia Project was named the **overall winner** of **The New Now : health**, an innovation competition organized by:

- The **Embassy of Switzerland in Greece**
- **Flux Laboratory Athens** / Fluxum Foundation
- **Inartis Foundation**

The award ceremony took place on **Friday, 3 December 2021** at the **Basil & Elise Goulandris / Theocharakis Foundation** in Athens, where finalists presented their improved projects and prototypes to a scientific jury of distinguished experts from various fields.

**Project lead:** Kyriakos Michailidis

📰 [Read the announcement (startupper.gr)](https://startupper.gr/news/78796/to-the-dyslexia-project-anadeichthike-nikitis-sto-the-new-nowhealth/)

---

## 🎯 About the Project

The Dyslexia Project aims to **improve the reading comprehension of people with dyslexia**. Dyslexia affects how people process written language, and this project set out to make reading more accessible and comprehensible for them.

---

## 🇨🇭 The Journey

As part of the competition, the finalists were **hosted in Switzerland for three months** by the Inartis Foundation. During this time they worked on developing their ideas under the supervision and support of a network of **2,000 specialist Swiss scientists**. Travel was kindly sponsored by **Aegean Airlines**.

---

## 💡 About The New Now : health

The New Now : health innovation competition aims to identify the **most creative idea** and reward concepts that leave a **positive social impact** in the field of health in Greece. The initiative encourages creativity and actively supports Greek entrepreneurship and innovation by helping to bring selected, distinguished ideas to life.
